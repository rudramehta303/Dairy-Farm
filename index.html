Our Legacy

Timeless Tradition

Dairy Farm Rajkot is a cherished name in the city, known for uncompromising quality in fresh dairy and artisanal sweet-making. Every batch of milk, paneer, and ghee carries our promise of purity.

We are more than just a dairy farm. Combining our own fresh dairy with age-old family recipes, we bring you traditional Indian sweets made the way they should be. Our coveted signature Bangle Sweets have become a staple for celebrations, loved for their irreplaceable taste and exceptional quality.
